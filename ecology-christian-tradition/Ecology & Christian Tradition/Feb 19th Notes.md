#### Correlative Theology
- An approach where existential questions about the human condition are correlated with Faith
- *In other words*, Find corollaries between questions that emerge within human culture, and how Christian communities respond to these questions. 
For example, "is the use of AI ethical?" How would **theology** be useful to investigate the use of AI?
