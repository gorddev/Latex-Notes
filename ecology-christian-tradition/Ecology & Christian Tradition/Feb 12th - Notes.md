### Some background on history of Ecology
Ecology is defined as the relationship between organisms and their environment. The word Eco comes from the "household." It's why the field of Ecology was contested as a discipline. 

Alexander Humboldt — How species do are how related to where they are and the status of their environment. This leads to discussion of a global climate.

After a lot more discussion, Ecology began being pursued as an actual disciple. 

Ernst Henkel, who was a friend of Darwin, defined the term **Ecology**. Expands upon the idea that *organisms are connected to each other*. › Not just living organisms, but all organisms. 

It's important to note that the formal recognition of Ecology is rather recent in human history.
You began to get Conservationist thinkers as well discussing the role of Ecology in the human condition. 

There's now even more attention put onto **Political Ecology**. Specifically how "Natural Ecology" and "Human Ecology" are related.
